10x Genomics · User Guide CG000731 Rev B

Published

NoteOverview

Barcoded Gel Beads, a cell suspension, and Partitioning Oil B are combined on a GEM-X 3’ Chip to generate nanoliter Gel Beads-in-emulsion (GEMs) at limiting dilution, so most GEMs contain either zero or one cell. Reverse transcription inside the GEMs produces barcoded, full-length cDNA from poly-adenylated mRNA. After breaking the emulsion, cDNA is cleaned up, amplified, and a fixed 25% carried forward into 3’ Gene Expression library construction (fragmentation, end repair/A-tailing, adaptor ligation, and sample index PCR), then sequenced.

Four steps: 1 GEM Generation & Barcoding → 2 Post GEM-RT Cleanup & cDNA Amplification → 3 3’ Gene Expression Library Construction → 4 Sequencing.

ImportantBefore starting
  • Equilibrate RT Reagent E, Reducing Agent B, cDNA Primers, Fragmentation Buffer, and Ligation Mix to room temperature before use.
  • Prepare cell suspension in 1X Phosphate-Buffered Saline (PBS); do not add PBS directly to the suspension — PBS is added to the Master Mix instead (Step 1.2c).
  • Prepare fresh 80% ethanol (15 mL per 8 reactions).
  • Thaw Cleanup Buffer for 10 min at 65°C; verify no visible crystals before use.
  • Vortex Dynabeads MyOne SILANE thoroughly (≥30 sec) immediately before adding to any mix — do not centrifuge beforehand.
  • Firmware ≥2.0.0 required for Chromium X/iX; ≥2.2.0 for Chromium Xo.

Step 1: GEM Generation & Barcoding

NoteObjective

GEMs are generated by combining barcoded Gel Beads, a Master Mix containing cells, and Partitioning Oil B on the GEM-X 3’ Chip. Cells are delivered at a limiting dilution so that the large majority of generated GEMs contain zero or one cell. Immediately after GEM generation, each Gel Bead dissolves and releases primers — Illumina TruSeq Read 1 + a 16 nt 10x Barcode + a 12 nt UMI + a 30 nt poly(dT) — which mix with the cell lysate and RT Master Mix. Incubating the GEMs then reverse-transcribes poly-adenylated mRNA into barcoded, full-length cDNA, with every transcript from a given GEM tagged with the same 10x Barcode.

NoteMath audit — reproduced from the source calculator table

10x Genomics ships this step as a large printed lookup table (Cell Stock Concentration × Targeted Cell Recovery, CG000731 p.34–35) rather than a formula. Back-calculating from >10 entries spanning both halves of that table gives a consistent relationship:

\[V_{cell\ suspension} = \frac{1.45 \times N_{target}}{C_{stock}} \qquad V_{PBS} = 36.6\;\mu L - V_{cell\ suspension}\]

where 36.6 µL is the fixed PBS + cell-suspension allowance (Master Mix 28.4 µL + 36.6 µL = 65 µL total per tube), and 1.45 is the recovery/loading factor implied by the table (≈69% capture efficiency). This reproduces every checked entry in the source table to within its printed rounding (±0.1 µL). If a computed cell suspension volume exceeds 36.6 µL, that combination isn’t achievable — the source table greys these cells out (“exceeds allowable PBS volume”); dilute your stock or lower the target instead.

Since stock concentration is entered per sample above, this section computes cell suspension and PBS volumes per sample too — each tube gets its own mix even though every tube targets the same cell recovery.

1.1 Prepare Master Mix

Assembles the RT enzyme, Template Switch Oligo, and Reducing Agent that will reverse-transcribe poly-adenylated mRNA into barcoded cDNA once combined with cells inside each GEM. Use the Master Mix table under “Calculations for this step” above.

  1. Prepare on ice, pipette mix 15x, centrifuge briefly. Add reagents in the listed order.
  2. Add 28.4 µL Master Mix into each tube of a PCR 8-tube strip on ice (1X per reaction, regardless of how many reactions the batch covers).

1.2 Load GEM-X Chip

Partitions cells + Master Mix, Gel Beads, and Partitioning Oil B into nanoliter-scale GEMs at limiting dilution, so most GEMs end up with zero or one cell. Uses the Cell Suspension Volume Calculator under “Calculations for this step” above.

b Vortex Gel Bead strip 30 sec in 10x Vortex Adapter; centrifuge ~5 sec; check for bubbles —
c For each tube, add that sample’s own PBS volume then its own cell suspension volume (per-sample table above) to its Master Mix; total 65 µL/tube for every sample Per-sample calculator above

WarningPartitioning Oil B is mandatory

Failure to add Partitioning Oil B to row 3 will prevent GEM generation and can damage the instrument. Run the chip on the Chromium X series instrument immediately after loading.

1.3 Run the Chromium X Series Instrument

Microfluidically drives cells, Gel Beads, and oil through the chip’s channels to form the single-cell GEM emulsion.

  1. Eject tray; place assembled chip with gasket, keeping it horizontal; retract tray.
  2. Confirm the GEM-X 3P program on screen; press play.
  3. Run takes ~6 min; instrument chimes at completion — proceed immediately.

1.4 Transfer GEMs

Recovers the GEM emulsion from the chip into a tube strip for thermal cycling. Abnormally high remaining volume in a recovery well is an early sign of a clog during GEM generation.

  1. Place a tube strip on ice.
  2. Eject chip; discard gasket; fold lid back to expose wells at 45°.
  3. Check rows 1–2 for abnormally high remaining volume (possible clog indicator).
  4. Slowly aspirate 100 µL GEMs from the lowest point of each recovery well (top NO FILL row) without sealing tips against the well bottom.
  5. Dispense into the tube strip on ice over ~20 sec, tips against the sidewalls.
  6. Start GEM incubation within 5 min. Route GEMs from separate chips to separate thermal cyclers if running back-to-back.

1.5 GEM-RT Incubation

Performs reverse transcription inside each intact GEM, so every resulting cDNA molecule from a given partition carries the same 10x Barcode + UMI pair.

Use a thermal cycler holding ≥100 µL (125 µL preferred, e.g. Bio-Rad C1000 Touch — use the highest available reaction volume setting on other instruments).

Step 2: Post GEM-RT Cleanup & cDNA Amplification

NoteObjective

After GEM-RT incubation, GEMs are broken and the pooled aqueous fraction is recovered. Silane magnetic beads (Dynabeads MyOne SILANE) then purify the first-strand barcoded cDNA away from the post GEM-RT reaction mixture, which still contains leftover RT enzyme, primers, and other biochemical reagents. The purified, barcoded, full-length cDNA is then amplified by PCR to generate enough mass for library construction.

2.1 Post GEM-RT Cleanup — Dynabeads

Breaks the GEM emulsion and uses silane magnetic beads to purify barcoded, full-length cDNA away from leftover RT enzyme, primers, and other post-RT reaction components.

  1. Add 125 µL Recovery Agent to each sample at room temperature. Do not pipette mix or vortex the biphasic mixture. Wait 2 min.
  2. Slowly remove and discard 125 µL Recovery Agent/Partitioning Oil (pink, bottom layer) — do not aspirate the aqueous phase.
  3. Prepare the Dynabeads Cleanup Mix (above); vortex SILANE beads ≥30 sec immediately before use; do not centrifuge.
  4. Vortex; add 200 µL to each tube; pipette mix 10x (pipette set to 200 µL).
  5. Incubate 10 min RT, caps open; re-mix at ~5 min to resuspend settled beads.
  6. Prepare Elution Solution I (above). Vortex and centrifuge briefly.

2.4 Post cDNA Amplification QC & Quantification

Determines total cDNA yield and mass, which sets the Sample Index PCR cycle count later in Step 3.5 — only a fixed 25% of this yield is carried forward into library construction regardless of the total (Step 3 Objective).

Run 1 µL (undiluted for low-RNA-content cells <1 pg total RNA/cell; 1:5–1:10 diluted for high-RNA-content cells) on a Bioanalyzer High Sensitivity chip (or TapeStation/LabChip). Select the ~200–9,000 bp region and read the reported concentration (pg/µL).

Step 3: 3’ Gene Expression Library Construction

NoteObjective

Enzymatic fragmentation and double-sided size selection optimize the cDNA amplicon size. P5 and P7 adaptors, i7 and i5 sample indexes, and a TruSeq Read 2 (read 2 primer sequence) are then added via End Repair, A-tailing, Adaptor Ligation, and PCR. The final libraries carry the P5 and P7 primers used for Illumina cluster generation and amplification.

NoteFixed-proportion input

Only 10 µL (25%) of the 40 µL total cDNA from Step 2.3m goes into fragmentation — regardless of total yield. This is deliberate: library complexity (median genes/UMIs per cell) rises sharply with cDNA input and then plateaus well before 100% of the cDNA is used, so carrying forward more than 25% buys little extra complexity for the material spent. The remaining 30 µL (75%) can be stored at 4°C (≤72 h) or −20°C (≤4 weeks) for additional libraries.

## Overview
Barcoded Gel Beads, a cell suspension, and Partitioning Oil B are combined on a GEM-X 3' Chip to generate nanoliter Gel Beads-in-emulsion (GEMs) at limiting dilution, so most GEMs contain either zero or one cell. Reverse transcription inside the GEMs produces barcoded, full-length cDNA from poly-adenylated mRNA. After breaking the emulsion, cDNA is cleaned up, amplified, and a fixed 25% carried forward into 3' Gene Expression library construction (fragmentation, end repair/A-tailing, adaptor ligation, and sample index PCR), then sequenced.

Four steps: **1** GEM Generation & Barcoding → **2** Post GEM-RT Cleanup & cDNA Amplification → **3** 3' Gene Expression Library Construction → **4** Sequencing.
:::

::: {.callout-important}
## Before starting
- Equilibrate RT Reagent E, Reducing Agent B, cDNA Primers, Fragmentation Buffer, and Ligation Mix to room temperature before use.
- Prepare cell suspension in 1X Phosphate-Buffered Saline (PBS); **do not** add PBS directly to the suspension — PBS is added to the Master Mix instead (Step 1.2c).
- Prepare fresh 80% ethanol (15 mL per 8 reactions).
- Thaw Cleanup Buffer for 10 min at 65°C; verify no visible crystals before use.
- Vortex Dynabeads MyOne SILANE thoroughly (≥30 sec) immediately before adding to any mix — **do not** centrifuge beforehand.
- Firmware ≥2.0.0 required for Chromium X/iX; ≥2.2.0 for Chromium Xo.
:::

## Step 1: GEM Generation & Barcoding

::: {.callout-note}
## Objective
GEMs are generated by combining barcoded Gel Beads, a Master Mix containing cells, and Partitioning Oil B on the GEM-X 3' Chip. Cells are delivered at a limiting dilution so that the large majority of generated GEMs contain zero or one cell. Immediately after GEM generation, each Gel Bead dissolves and releases primers — Illumina TruSeq Read 1 + a 16 nt 10x Barcode + a 12 nt UMI + a 30 nt poly(dT) — which mix with the cell lysate and RT Master Mix. ## Math audit — reproduced from the source calculator table
10x Genomics ships this step as a large printed lookup table (Cell Stock Concentration × Targeted Cell Recovery, CG000731 p.34–35) rather than a formula. Back-calculating from >10 entries spanning both halves of that table gives a consistent relationship:

$$V_{cell\ suspension} = \frac{1.45 \times N_{target}}{C_{stock}} \qquad V_{PBS} = 36.6\;\mu L - V_{cell\ suspension}$$

where 36.6 µL is the fixed PBS + cell-suspension allowance (Master Mix 28.4 µL + 36.6 µL = 65 µL total per tube), and 1.45 is the recovery/loading factor implied by the table (≈69% capture efficiency). This reproduces every checked entry in the source table to within its printed rounding (±0.1 µL). If a computed cell suspension volume exceeds 36.6 µL, that combination isn't achievable — the source table greys these cells out ("exceeds allowable PBS volume"); dilute your stock or lower the target instead.

Since stock concentration is entered **per sample** above, this section computes cell suspension and PBS volumes **per sample** too — each tube gets its own mix even though every tube targets the same cell recovery.

### 1.4 Transfer GEMs
*Recovers the GEM emulsion from the chip into a tube strip for thermal cycling. Abnormally high remaining volume in a recovery well is an early sign of a clog during GEM generation.*

a. Place a tube strip on ice.

d. Slowly aspirate **100 µL** GEMs from the lowest point of each recovery well (top NO FILL row) without sealing tips against the well bottom.
e. Dispense into the tube strip on ice over ~20 sec, tips against the sidewalls.
f. Start GEM incubation within 5 min. Route GEMs from separate chips to separate thermal cyclers if running back-to-back.

*Determines total cDNA yield and mass, which sets the Sample Index PCR cycle count later in Step 3.5 — only a fixed 25% of this yield is carried forward into library construction regardless of the total (Step 3 Objective).*

:::

## Step 3: 3' Gene Expression Library Construction

::: {.callout-note}
## Objective
Enzymatic fragmentation and double-sided size selection optimize the cDNA amplicon size. P5 and P7 adaptors, i7 and i5 sample indexes, and a TruSeq Read 2 (read 2 primer sequence) are then added via End Repair, A-tailing, Adaptor Ligation, and PCR. The final libraries carry the P5 and P7 primers used for Illumina cluster generation and amplification.
:::

::: {.callout-note}
## Fixed-proportion input
Only **10 µL (25%)** of the 40 µL total cDNA from Step 2.3m goes into fragmentation — regardless of total yield. This is deliberate: library complexity (median genes/UMIs per cell) rises sharply with cDNA input and then plateaus well before 100% of the cDNA is used, so carrying forward more than 25% buys little extra complexity for the material spent. The remaining 30 µL (75%) can be stored at 4°C (≤72 h) or −20°C (≤4 weeks) for additional libraries.
:::

## Step 4: Sequencing

::: {.callout-note}
## Objective
A Chromium Single Cell 3' Gene Expression Dual Index library is a standard Illumina paired-end construct beginning and ending with P5 and P7. The 16 bp 10x Barcode and 12 bp UMI are encoded in Read 1, while Read 2 sequences the cDNA fragment itself. i7 and i5 index sequences are read as separate sample index reads for demultiplexing. TruSeq Read 1 and TruSeq Read 2 are the standard Illumina sequencing primer sites used for paired-end sequencing.
:::

**Library depth & run parameters**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Sequencing depth | Minimum 20,000 read pairs/cell |
| Sequencing type | Paired-end, dual indexing |
| Read 1 | 28 cycles |
| i7 Index | 10 cycles |
| i5 Index | 10 cycles |
| Read 2 | 90 cycles |

**Loading concentration by instrument**

| Instrument | Loading conc. (pM)* | PhiX (%) |
|------------|---------------------|----------|
| MiSeq | 12 | 1 |
| NextSeq 500/550 | 1.6 | 1 |
| NextSeq 1000/2000 | 650 | 1 |
| HiSeq 2500 (RR) | 12 | 1 |
| HiSeq 4000 | 240 | 1 |
| NovaSeq 6000 (Standard & Xp) | 150 | 1 |

\*Check the 10x Genomics Support site for current loading concentrations — these are periodically updated.

::: {.callout-warning}
## Index hopping mitigation
Store each library individually (4°C ≤72 h, or −20°C long-term) — **do not** pool during storage. Pool only immediately before sequencing; an extra 1.0X SPRI cleanup on the pooled libraries can remove residual free adaptors. Never pool two libraries sharing the same sample index in one lane — this breaks demultiplexing.
:::

Compatible sequencers: MiSeq, NextSeq 500/550/2000, HiSeq 2500 (Rapid Run), HiSeq 3000/4000, NovaSeq 6000. Data is analyzed with Cell Ranger (BCL/FASTQ → BAM, MEX, CSV, HDF5, web summary, .cloupe) and visualized in Loupe Browser.
