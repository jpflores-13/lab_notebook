PMA-induced M0 macrophage differentiation, then M1 (LPS + IFNγ) / M2 (IL-4 + IL-13) polarization

Published

September 9, 2026

NoteOverview

THP-1 monocytes are differentiated into resting M0 macrophages with phorbol 12-myristate 13-acetate (PMA), rested, then split into unstimulated (M0), classically-activated (M1, lipopolysaccharide (LPS) + interferon gamma (IFNγ)), and alternatively-activated (M2, interleukin-4 (IL-4) + interleukin-13 (IL-13)) arms.

This page combines three source documents:

  • Differentiation_THP1s.docx — an older, vessel-agnostic PMA differentiation write-up (T-175 flask scale, fill-in-the-blank worksheet style). Used below as the legacy/flask-scale workflow.
  • THP1_Polarization_Protocol.docx — the current, validated day-by-day 6-well-plate workflow carrying M0 macrophages through to M1 and M2 polarization. Used below as the standard workflow.
  • THP1_Polarization_Volumes.xlsx — a 3-sheet vessel-volume calculator (PMA Calc, LPS Calc, IFNg Calc) reproduced as the reactive calculator in this page. Its worked example (6-well plate, defaults below) reproduces every volume printed in THP1_Polarization_Protocol.docx’s vessel table exactly, so the two source documents are internally consistent.
ImportantBefore starting
  • Prepare all stocks and further dilutions under a chemical fume hood.
  • PMA is a tumor promoter/protein kinase C activator — handle stock and working dilutions with gloves and avoid skin contact.
  • LPS (lipopolysaccharide) is a bacterial endotoxin — treat as a biohazard reagent per your institution’s guidelines.
  • All cytokine/PMA aliquots are stored at −20°C; the working PMA and LPS dilutions below are made fresh from frozen aliquots on the day of use.
  • Warm RPMI/FCS media to 37°C before adding to cells; do not warm PMA, LPS, or cytokine stocks above room temperature.

TipReproducing the source calculator (math audit)

All volumes below are derived, never hardcoded, from the same relationship used in THP1_Polarization_Volumes.xlsx:

\[V_{stock\ to\ add\ (\mu L)} = \frac{C_{final} \times (V_{vessel,\ mL} \times 1000)}{C_{stock\ used}}\]

PMA’s stock concentration is back-calculated from mass and molarity:

\[C_{PMA\ stock} = \frac{mass\ (g)/volume\ (L)}{MW_{PMA}\ (616.83\ g/mol)}, \qquad C_{working} = \frac{C_{stock}}{10}\ \text{(1:10 dilution)}\]

LPS uses one of two serial 1:100 dilutions of its 1 mg/mL stock depending on vessel size — smaller vessels (24-well, 6-well) draw from the more dilute “2x dil” stock (100 ng/mL) so pipetting volumes stay reasonable; larger vessels (T-25/T-175/T-525) draw from the “1x dil” stock (10 µg/mL). This matches vessel_table.lps_dilution above.

Plugging in the defaults (6-well, PMA 25 nM, LPS 10 ng/mL, IFNγ 20 ng/mL) reproduces the source spreadsheet and THP1_Polarization_Protocol.docx’s printed table (7.72 µL PMA, 500 µL LPS, 10 µL IFNγ) exactly.

Stock solution prep

Make once; store single-use aliquots at −20°C. Scale the whole batch with the multiplier above if you need more aliquots than the standard recipe yields.

PMA stock — 100 µg/mL (162 µM) in DMSO

Code 
pma_MW              = 616.83
pma_mass_mg         = 1.0 * stock_mult
pma_dmso_mL         = 10.0 * stock_mult
pma_conc_gL         = pma_mass_mg / pma_dmso_mL           // mg/mL == g/L
pma_molarity_uM     = (pma_conc_gL / pma_MW) * 1000
pma_stock_aliquot_uL = 50
pma_stock_aliquots  = (pma_dmso_mL * 1000) / pma_stock_aliquot_uL

Inputs.table([
  {Component: "12-O-tetradecanoylphorbol-13-acetate (PMA), lyophilized", Amount: `${fmt(pma_mass_mg,1)} mg` },
  {Component: "Dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO)", Amount: `${fmt(pma_dmso_mL,1)} mL` },
  {Component: "Resulting stock concentration", Amount: `100 µg/mL (${fmt(pma_molarity_uM,1)} µM)` },
  {Component: "Single-use aliquots", Amount: `${fmt(pma_stock_aliquots,0)} aliquots of ${pma_stock_aliquot_uL} µL, −20°C` }
], {layout: "auto"})
TipWorking (1:10) PMA dilution

Per THP1_Polarization_Protocol.docx, the working dilution is made fresh in RPMI/FCS media (not DMSO) immediately before treating cells: 1:10 dilution of the 100 µg/mL stock in RPMI/FCS → 10 µg/mL working stock.

Code 
pma_working_stock_nM = pma_molarity_uM * 1000 / 10   // 1:10 dilution, µM → nM

md`**Working PMA stock (1:10 in RPMI/FCS): ≈${fmt(pma_working_stock_nM,0)} nM** — this is the value used as $C_{stock}$ for the vessel calculator below.`

IFNγ (interferon gamma) stock — 10 µg/mL in PBS + BSA carrier

NoteBSA carrier concentration — flagged discrepancy

The source spreadsheet labels this carrier “PBS + 1% BSA,” but back-calculating from the docx recipe (100 µL of 20 mg/mL BSA into a 2.0 mL final volume) gives 2 mg BSA / 2.0 mL = 0.1% w/v, not 1%. The same 20 mg/mL BSA stock and dilution ratio is used for the IL-4 and IL-13 carriers below, which also work out to 0.1% w/v.

Code

ifng_large_aliquot_uL  = 200

## Reproducing the source calculator (math audit)
All volumes below are derived, never hardcoded, from the same relationship used in `THP1_Polarization_Volumes.xlsx`:

$$V_{stock\ to\ add\ (\mu L)} = \frac{C_{final} \times (V_{vessel,\ mL} \times 1000)}{C_{stock\ used}}$$

PMA's stock concentration is back-calculated from mass and molarity:

$$C_{PMA\ stock} = \frac{mass\ (g)/volume\ (L)}{MW_{PMA}\ (616.83\ g/mol)}, \qquad C_{working} = \frac{C_{stock}}{10}\ \text{(1:10 dilution)}$$

LPS uses one of two serial 1:100 dilutions of its 1 mg/mL stock depending on vessel size — smaller vessels (24-well, 6-well) draw from the more dilute "2x dil" stock (100 ng/mL) so pipetting volumes stay reasonable; larger vessels (T-25/T-175/T-525) draw from the "1x dil" stock (10 µg/mL). This matches `vessel_table.lps_dilution` above.

Plugging in the defaults (6-well, PMA 25 nM, LPS 10 ng/mL, IFNγ 20 ng/mL) reproduces the source spreadsheet and `THP1_Polarization_Protocol.docx`'s printed table (7.72 µL PMA, 500 µL LPS, 10 µL IFNγ) exactly.
