QuickExtract DNA prep + EconoTaq PCR genotyping of single-clone colonies

Published

September 9, 2026

NoteOverview

Genomic DNA extraction and PCR genotyping of single-clone expansion colonies, at least 4 weeks after single-clone seeding. Part A extracts crude genomic DNA directly from a small culture volume using Epicentre QuickExtract™ DNA Extraction Solution 1.0 (no column cleanup). Part B PCR-genotypes that DNA with EconoTaq® PLUS GREEN 2X Master Mix and clone-specific primers, visualized on a 2% agarose gel.

ImportantBefore starting
  • Thaw QuickExtract (QE) Buffer before starting.
  • Set centrifuge to room temperature, 8000 rpm.
  • All PCR preparation (Part B) should be completed on ice.
  • Have your 96-well V-bottom plate and 8-well PCR tube strips ready.

Configure genotyping run

Code 
viewof n_clones    = Inputs.number({label: "Number of clones (wells) to genotype", value: 24, min: 1, max: 96, step: 1})
viewof excess_pct  = Inputs.number({label: "Master mix overage (%)", value: 10, min: 0, max: 30, step: 5})
Code 
scale = n_clones * (1 + excess_pct / 100)
fmt   = (x, d = 1) => x.toFixed(d)

md`**${n_clones} clone(s) · ${excess_pct}% overage · scale factor ${fmt(scale, 2)}×**`
Code 
n_clones > 96
  ? md`::: {.callout-warning}
**Exceeds one 96-well plate.** ${n_clones} clones will not fit in a single 96-well V-bottom plate — split the extraction across multiple plates.
:::`
  : md``
NoteMath audit — how every volume is calculated

Every scaled volume below uses the same linear formula:

\[V_{total} = V_{per\_well} \times N_{clones} \times (1 + overage)\]

QuickExtract Buffer (30 µL/well) and the PCR master mix components both scale this way. Template DNA (QE DNA) is not part of the master mix — it differs per clone and is added individually to each reaction.

Part A: DNA extraction

Code 
qe_vol_per_well = 30   // µL QuickExtract Buffer per well
qe_total        = qe_vol_per_well * scale

md`**QuickExtract Buffer needed: ${fmt(qe_total)} µL** (${qe_vol_per_well} µL/well × ${n_clones} clone(s), ${excess_pct}% overage)`
Step Action Volume
1 In tissue culture hood, pipet culture up and down to mix —
2 Transfer culture volume to an appropriate 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube 1 mL/sample
3 Centrifuge 8000 rpm, 5 min, room temperature —
4 Carefully tilt tube and pipet off supernatant — do not disturb the cell pellet —
5 Wash with Phosphate-Buffered Saline (PBS) 1 mL/sample
6 Centrifuge 8000 rpm, 5 min, room temperature —
7 Carefully tilt tube and pipet off supernatant — do not disturb the cell pellet —
8 Add QuickExtract buffer per well — do not mix 30 µL/well
9 Place at 37°C for 10 min —
10 Pipet cell suspension up and down —
11 Collect suspension; transfer to 8-well PCR tube strips —
12 Vortex 15 sec; briefly centrifuge —
13 Place in thermocycler at 65°C for 6 min —
14 Vortex 15 sec; briefly centrifuge —
15 Place in thermocycler at 98°C for 2 min —
16 Move on to Part B, or store samples at −20°C —
TipStopping point

Extracted DNA is stable at −20°C if you need to pause between Part A and Part B.

Part B: PCR

Important

All PCR preparation should be completed on ice.

Master mix

Code 
pcr_components = [
  {Component: "2X EconoTaq PLUS GREEN Master Mix", "Per reaction (µL)": 10},
  {Component: "Primer A (10 µM)",                   "Per reaction (µL)": 2},
  {Component: "Primer B (10 µM)",                   "Per reaction (µL)": 2},
  {Component: "Nuclease-Free Water",                "Per reaction (µL)": 4}
]
mm_per_rxn = pcr_components.reduce((s, r) => s + r["Per reaction (µL)"], 0)

Inputs.table(
  pcr_components
    .map(r => ({ ...r, [`${fmt(scale, 2)}× + overage (µL)`]: fmt(r["Per reaction (µL)"] * scale) }))
    .concat([{ Component: "Total master mix", "Per reaction (µL)": mm_per_rxn, [`${fmt(scale, 2)}× + overage (µL)`]: fmt(mm_per_rxn * scale) }]),
  { layout: "auto" }
)

Add 18 µL master mix + 2 µL QE DNA (template, added individually per well) = 20 µL total reaction — reproducing the source’s 20 µL reaction table exactly.

TipPipetting order

Add items to each PCR reaction in the order listed: master mix components first, then QE DNA last, on ice.

Thermocycler program: “Econotaq – Genotyping”

Cycle step Temp. Time Cycles
Initial denaturation 98°C 30 s 1
Denaturation / Annealing / Extension 98°C / primer Tm / 72°C 30 s / 30 s / 2 min 25
Final extension / Hold 72°C / 4°C 10 min / hold 1

Visualize on a 2% agarose gel.
