iScript cDNA Synthesis Kit (Bio-Rad, cat #1708891)

Published

September 9, 2026

NoteOverview

Reverse-transcribes extracted RNA into cDNA using the iScript cDNA Synthesis Kit. RNA input volume is back-calculated from your sample’s measured concentration to hit a fixed target mass in a 15 µL final reaction volume, then a 5x iScript Reaction Mix + iScript Reverse Transcriptase master mix is added and run on a 3-step thermal cycler protocol.

ImportantBefore starting
  • Have your RNA concentration (ng/µL, e.g. from Nanodrop or Qubit) for each sample on hand — needed for the input volume calculator below.
  • Prepare the iScript master mix fresh, on ice, immediately before use.
  • Pre-program the thermal cycler with the 4-step protocol below.

RNA input calculator

NoteMath audit — how the RNA/water volumes are calculated

The source protocol reads: “Calculate the volume of RNA needed for 1g and add water to 15µl final volume” — “1g” is almost certainly a typo for 1 µg (a target RNA mass of 1 gram is not physically sensible for a bench RNA extraction; 1 µg is the standard iScript input and matches the embedded calculator spreadsheet found inside the source .docx, which computes =1000/[conc. ng/µL] — i.e. µL needed for 1000 ng = 1 µg). This is corrected below; flagging visibly per house convention rather than silently changing the source or preserving the apparent error.

\[V_{RNA} = \frac{Mass_{target}\;(ng)}{C_{RNA}\;(ng/\mu L)} \qquad V_{water} = V_{final} - V_{RNA}\]

If \(V_{RNA} > V_{final}\), the target mass is not reachable at that final volume and concentration — dilute less, use more RNA-concentrated input, or accept a lower input mass.

Code 
viewof target_mass_ug = Inputs.number({label: "Target RNA mass (µg) — corrected from source's \"1g\"", value: 1, min: 0.01, step: 0.1})
viewof rna_conc_ng_uL  = Inputs.number({label: "RNA concentration (ng/µL)", value: 100, min: 0.1, step: 1})
Code 
rna_vol_uL   = (target_mass_ug * 1000) / rna_conc_ng_uL
water_vol_uL = final_vol_uL - rna_vol_uL

rna_vol_uL > final_vol_uL
  ? md`::: {.callout-warning}
**Not reachable at this concentration.** ${fmt(rna_vol_uL)} µL of RNA would be needed to hit ${target_mass_ug} µg, which exceeds the ${final_vol_uL} µL final volume. Use a higher-concentration RNA prep, lower the target mass, or increase the final volume.
:::`
  : Inputs.table([
      {Component: "RNA sample", "Volume (µL)": fmt(rna_vol_uL)},
      {Component: "Nuclease-free water", "Volume (µL)": fmt(water_vol_uL)},
      {Component: `Total (in PCR tube, per sample)`, "Volume (µL)": fmt(final_vol_uL)}
    ], {layout: "auto"})

Aliquot 5 µL of master mix into each PCR tube. Be careful not to touch the samples with the tip — change tips between samples if this happens.

Protocol

Step Action Detail
1 Calculate RNA volume for your target mass; add nuclease-free water to final volume in a PCR tube See calculator above
2 Prepare 5x iScript Reaction Mix + iScript Reverse Transcriptase master mix See table above
3 Aliquot 5 µL master mix into each PCR tube Avoid touching sample; change tips if contacted
4 Run the complete reaction mix on a thermal cycler See program below
5 Store cDNA −20°C

## Math audit — how the RNA/water volumes are calculated
The source protocol reads: *"Calculate the volume of RNA needed for 1g and add water to 15µl final volume"* — **"1g" is almost certainly a typo for 1 µg** (a target RNA mass of 1 gram is not physically sensible for a bench RNA extraction; 1 µg is the standard iScript input and matches the embedded calculator spreadsheet found inside the source `.docx`, which computes `=1000/[conc. ng/µL]` — i.e. µL needed for 1000 ng = 1 µg). This is corrected below; flagging visibly per house convention rather than silently changing the source or preserving the apparent error.

$$V_{RNA} = \frac{Mass_{target}\;(ng)}{C_{RNA}\;(ng/\mu L)} \qquad V_{water} = V_{final} - V_{RNA}$$

If $V_{RNA} > V_{final}$, the target mass is not reachable at that final volume and concentration — dilute less, use more RNA-concentrated input, or accept a lower input mass.
:::
