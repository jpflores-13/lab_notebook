STEMdiff Hematopoietic → Microglia Differentiation → Microglia Maturation

Published

September 9, 2026

NoteOverview

Human iPSCs are carried through hematopoietic progenitor cell (HPC) differentiation (STEMdiff Hematopoietic Kit), then microglia differentiation, then microglia maturation, yielding iPSC-derived microglia-like cells (iMGLs) ready for treatment/assay by ~Day 40–46. Source: iMGLs_Protocol.docx.

WarningDay-numbering in the source document

The source document’s own “Order of iMGL Protocol” outline (reproduced below) restarts or overlaps day counts between phases — most clearly flagged where it explicitly marks the HPC phase start as “Day 12/Day 0,” i.e. absolute Day 12 of the whole protocol is simultaneously “Day 0” of the STEMdiff Hematopoietic Kit’s own day count. The Microglia Differentiation and Maturation phases are not given this same dual-labeling in the outline, but the body text’s own day labels within each phase (“Day 12: harvest HPCs,” “Day 24: transfer suspension,” “Day 36: proceed to maturation,” “Day 40–46: mature”) are internally consistent with each other and are what this page uses throughout.

Order of iMGL protocol (source outline, reproduced)

Phase Day Milestone
iPSC maintenance 0 Thaw iPSCs into 1 well
iPSC maintenance 1 Change media
iPSC maintenance 2–4 Scrape for bad colonies
iPSC maintenance 5 Split iPSCs into 6 wells
iPSC maintenance 6 Change media
iPSC maintenance 7–9 Scrape for bad colonies
iPSC maintenance 10 Split iPSCs into 3 different concentrations
iPSC maintenance 11 Change media
HPC differentiation 12 / HPC Day 0 Confirm aggregates; start HPC phase
HPC differentiation 12 (HPC Day 12) Harvest HPCs
Microglia differentiation 12 → 24 STEMdiff Microglia Differentiation Medium
Microglia maturation 36 → 40–46 STEMdiff Microglia Maturation Medium
Treatment 40+ Treat iMGLs

iPSC maintenance phase (Day 0–11)

ImportantUses mTeSR1 + Thiazovivin — not plain TeSR-E8

The microglia-differentiation-track iPSCs in this protocol are maintained in mTeSR™1 (STEMCELL cat#: 5990/85857) with Thiazovivin (a ROCK inhibitor, 1000X aliquot, 1 µL/mL final) added to media used for thawing and splitting — not the plain TeSR™-E8 used in the base iPSC culture protocol. For Matrigel coating, thawing mechanics, and general passaging technique, see that page — it is not repeated here.

The 15/45-minute incubator rule from the base iPSC protocol applies here too.

Day 5 / Day 10 — “Splitting” step (distinct from full passaging)

This track’s outline calls for a lighter-weight splitting step at Day 5 and Day 10 (scan/scrape bad colonies, lift with mTeSR1 + Thiazovivin, replate at 1–2 new densities) rather than the full EDTA passage described in the base protocol:

TipAttributed notes

Note from Isha: Day 1 after plating, the iPSCs are usually very stressed, so a media change will suffice. Scanning and scraping for bad colonies should start on Day 2. This note applies to the iPSC splits as well — the more crowded the plate, the easier it is to pick up the cells.

Note from Justin: If a lot of single-cell differentiation is seen, collect everything into a Falcon tube and centrifuge at 200×g for 30 sec. The aggregates pellet while single differentiated cells remain in the supernatant — discard the supernatant and culture the pellet.

HPC differentiation phase (STEMdiff™ Hematopoietic Kit, Day 0–12)

NoteMedia prep math audit

Table 1 of the STEMdiff™ Hematopoietic Kit insert specifies Medium A (45 mL Basal + 225 µL Supplement A) and Medium B (75 mL Basal + 375 µL Supplement B) — both a 1:200 (200X) supplement dilution (\(225/45{,}000 = 375/75{,}000 = 1/200\)). The calculator below applies that same 1:200 ratio to whatever basal-medium batch size you enter.

mediumB_supp_uL = mediumB_basal_mL * 1000 * supplement_ratio

Store complete medium at 2–8°C for up to 3 weeks, or −20°C for up to 6 months. Thaw Basal Medium at RT or overnight at 2–8°C; thaw Supplement A/B at RT or 2–8°C (centrifuge 30 sec if needed to remove liquid from the cap). Warm all media to RT before use; do not leave at RT for extended periods.

Passaging cells as aggregates & setting up HPC differentiation

  1. Prepare Matrigel-coated plates (see iPSC culture protocol).
  2. Perform triplicate aggregate counts: draw a “+” counting grid centered on the bottom of 3 wells of a 96-well flat-bottom plate. Aliquot 40 µL DMEM/F12 into each of the 3 wells, then add 5 µL of aggregate mixture to each. Count aggregates ≥50 µm diameter in each well; average the triplicate to get the Concentration of Cell Aggregates.
  3. Plate 96–196 aggregates/well (10–20 aggregates/cm²) to achieve 40–80 colonies/well (4–10 colonies/cm²) adhered after 24 h — multiple plating densities may need to be tested.
TipPlating volume calculator

\[Plating\ Volume\ (\mu L) = \frac{Number\ of\ Aggregates\ to\ Plate}{Concentration\ of\ Cell\ Aggregates\ (aggregates/\mu L)}\]

Code 
viewof n_aggregates_to_plate = Inputs.number({label: "Number of aggregates to plate (source range: 96–196)", value: 146, min: 96, max: 196, step: 1})
viewof aggregate_conc_per_uL = Inputs.number({label: "Your measured concentration of cell aggregates (aggregates/µL, from the triplicate count above)", value: 5, min: 0.1, step: 0.1})
Code 
plating_volume_uL = n_aggregates_to_plate / aggregate_conc_per_uL

md`**Plating volume: ${fmt(plating_volume_uL,1)} µL** of aggregate mixture per well — add to a Matrigel-coated 12-well plate containing 1 mL mTeSR1. If using split ratios instead of a counted plating volume, 1:40 to 1:200 may be required depending on confluence.`
  1. Gently mix the aggregate mixture; add the calculated plating volume to each Matrigel-coated well (1 mL mTeSR1 already in the well).
  2. Place in the 37°C incubator; move in short back-and-forth/side-to-side motions to distribute aggregates; do not disturb for 24 h. Change media with mTeSR1 after 24 h; do not disturb for another 24 h. Count to confirm aggregates after 48 h.

Stage 1 — Day 0–2

WarningColony-count thresholds — discrepancy flagged in the source

The source states two different lower bounds in adjacent paragraphs: “Confirm that 80–160 colonies/well are adhered” (4–10 colonies/cm²), but then “CRITICAL: Do not proceed if cultures have < 40 colonies or > 160 colonies per well.” Both numbers are reproduced verbatim below.

Day Step
0 Confirm 80–160 colonies/well adhered (4–10 colonies/cm²) — count all colonies, including tiny few-cell ones. To ease counting, replace medium with fresh mTeSR™1/mTeSR™ Plus/TeSR™-E8 first (removes debris). CRITICAL: do not proceed if < 40 or > 160 colonies/well (see discrepancy note above) — differentiation will be compromised.
0 Aspirate medium; add 2 mL Medium A/well. Incubate 37°C for 2 days.
2 Using a serological or 1 mL pipette, gently remove 1 mL medium/well; discard. Gently add 1 mL Medium A/well. Incubate 37°C for 24 h.

Stage 2 — Day 3–12

Day Step
3 Aspirate medium; gently add 2 mL Medium B/well. Incubate 37°C for 2 days.
5 Gently remove 1 mL medium/well; discard. Gently add 1 mL Medium B/well. Incubate 37°C for 2 days.
7 Floating cells become visible and increase for the rest of the protocol. Keeping the plate flat, gently remove 1 mL medium/well without disturbing the floating population; discard. Add 1 mL Medium B/well. Incubate 37°C for 3 days.
10 Keeping the plate flat, gently remove 1 mL medium/well; discard. Add 1 mL Medium B/well. Incubate 37°C for 2 days.
12 Harvest hematopoietic cells (see below).
TipOptional Day 10 harvest

Cells may be harvested at Day 10 instead, as described for Day 12. Yield and the proportion of CD34+CD45+ cells will be lower than at Day 12, but CFUs may be present at a higher frequency.

NoteMedia “total required” figures don’t match the per-day add volumes

The source states Medium A total required is “1.5 mL per well” (for Day 0 + Day 2) and Medium B total required is “2.5 mL per well” (for Day 3, 5, 7, 10) — but summing the actual per-day add volumes gives 3 mL/well for Medium A (2 mL + 1 mL) and 5 mL/well for Medium B (2 + 1 + 1 + 1 mL), exactly double the stated totals in both cases.

Code 
mediumA_per_well_mL = 3     // 2 mL (Day 0) + 1 mL (Day 2) — see discrepancy note above
mediumB_per_well_mL = 5     // 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 mL (Day 3, 5, 7, 10) — see discrepancy note above

Inputs.table([
  {Medium: "Medium A", "Per well (summed)": `${mediumA_per_well_mL} mL`, "Total (this batch)": `${fmt(mediumA_per_well_mL * n_wells_hpc,1)} mL`},
  {Medium: "Medium B", "Per well (summed)": `${mediumB_per_well_mL} mL`, "Total (this batch)": `${fmt(mediumB_per_well_mL * n_wells_hpc,1)} mL`}
], {layout: "auto"})

Day 12 — Harvest hematopoietic cells

Step Action
1 Vigorously pipette (triturate) cells up/down in the well to break them up as needed.
2 Transfer the cell suspension to a collection tube.
3 Add 1 mL DMEM/F-12 to the well; triturate vigorously and add to the collection tube.
4 Repeat step 3.
5 Centrifuge 300×g, 5 min, RT. Discard the supernatant.
6 Resuspend the cell pellet in the desired medium for analysis or downstream assays.

Microglia differentiation phase (Day 12–36)

NoteMedia prep math audit

Add 10 mL Supplement 1 and 400 µL Supplement 2 to 90 mL Basal Medium — ratios of 10/90 and 400/90 per mL of basal, applied to whatever batch size you enter below.

Code 
viewof diff_basal_mL = Inputs.number({label: "STEMdiff™ Microglia Differentiation — Basal Medium batch (mL)", value: 90, min: 1, step: 1})
Code 
diff_supp1_mL = diff_basal_mL * 10/90
diff_supp2_uL = diff_basal_mL * 400/90

Inputs.table([

  {Component: "Supplement 1", Volume: `${fmt(diff_supp1_mL,2)} mL`},
  {Component: "Supplement 2", Volume: `${fmt(diff_supp2_uL,0)} µL`}
], {layout: "auto"})

Store at 2–8°C for up to 4 weeks; warm to 37°C before use.

Code 
viewof diff_seed_cells = Inputs.number({label: "Cells seeded per well (source range: 1×10⁵–2×10⁵)", value: 150000, min: 100000, max: 200000, step: 10000})
Code 
diff_seed_density_per_cm2 = diff_seed_cells / well_area_cm2

md`**Seeding density: ${fmt(diff_seed_density_per_cm2,0)} cells/cm²** (source target range: 1.1×10⁴–2.2×10⁴ cells/cm² for a 6-well plate, ${well_area_cm2} cm²/well)`
Day Step
12 Harvest suspended HPCs (Day 12 of the HPC phase above); count with Trypan Blue + hemocytometer.
12 Add the seeded cell number (above) to 1 well of a Matrigel-coated 6-well plate containing 2 mL STEMdiff™ Microglia Differentiation Medium. Incubate 37°C, 5% CO₂.
every other day Feed by topping up with half the start volume (1 mL) of medium — do not remove existing medium.
24 Transfer the entire cell suspension to a 15 mL conical; centrifuge 300×g, 5 min. Remove supernatant, leaving ~1 mL on top of the pellet; gently resuspend.
24 Add the cell suspension to 1 well of the old Matrigel-coated plate (not a new one) containing 1 mL fresh medium. Incubate.
every other day, ×12 days Feed by topping up with half the start volume (1 mL).
36 Proceed to microglia maturation.

Microglia maturation phase (Day 36–46)

NoteMedia prep math audit

Add 10 mL Supplement 1, 400 µL Supplement 2, and 400 µL Supplement 3 to 90 mL Basal Medium — same ratios as the Differentiation Medium above, plus Supplement 3 at the same ratio as Supplement 2.

Code 
viewof mat_basal_mL = Inputs.number({label: "STEMdiff™ Microglia Maturation — Basal Medium batch (mL)", value: 90, min: 1, step: 1})
Code 
mat_supp1_mL = mat_basal_mL * 10/90
mat_supp2_uL = mat_basal_mL * 400/90
mat_supp3_uL = mat_basal_mL * 400/90

Inputs.table([
  {Component: "STEMdiff™ Microglia Basal Medium", Volume: `${fmt(mat_basal_mL,1)} mL`},
  {Component: "Supplement 1", Volume: `${fmt(mat_supp1_mL,2)} mL`},
  {Component: "Supplement 2", Volume: `${fmt(mat_supp2_uL,0)} µL`},
  {Component: "Supplement 3", Volume: `${fmt(mat_supp3_uL,0)} µL`}
], {layout: "auto"})

Store at 2–8°C for up to 4 weeks; warm to 37°C before use.

Code 
viewof mat_replate_cells = Inputs.number({label: "Cells replated (source target: 1×10⁶; minimum viable: 5×10⁵)", value: 1000000, min: 100000, step: 50000})
Code 
mat_replate_density_per_cm2 = mat_replate_cells / well_area_cm2
mat_below_min = mat_replate_cells < 500000

mat_below_min
  ? md`::: {.callout-warning}
**${fmt(mat_replate_cells,0)} cells is below the 5×10⁵ minimum.** Replating density = ${fmt(mat_replate_density_per_cm2,0)} cells/cm² — continuing with fewer than 5×10⁵ cells (5.5×10⁴ cells/cm²) may reduce the %CD11b+ yield at the end of maturation.
:::`
  : md`**Replating density: ${fmt(mat_replate_density_per_cm2,0)} cells/cm²** (optimal target: 1×10⁵ cells/cm²).`
Day Step
36 Transfer the entire cell suspension to a 15 mL conical.
36 Add 1 mL/well 1× PBS/EDTA; incubate 4 min. Collect and transfer to the conical. Wash with another 1 mL/well PBS/EDTA; collect and add to the conical.
36 Centrifuge 300×g, 5 min. Remove supernatant, leaving ~1 mL on top of the pellet; gently resuspend. Count with Trypan Blue + hemocytometer.
36 Add the replating cell number (above) to 1 well of the old Matrigel plate (not new) containing 1 mL fresh Maturation Medium (target 1×10⁵ cells/cm²). If total well volume < 2 mL, top up to 2 mL. Incubate 37°C, 5% CO₂.
every other day Feed by topping up with half the start volume (1 mL).
40–46 Microglia are mature after 4–10 days in Maturation Medium. Limited capacity for expansion; cell death may increase after 10 days of culture.
TipImmunocytochemistry cultureware

If cells are for immunocytochemistry, use cultureware coated with either 10 µg/mL PDL or 1 µg/mL fibronectin instead of Matrigel. For other applications, use Matrigel-coated cultureware.

Freezing hematopoietic progenitor cells

Two methods are given in the source — present both, since they specify different freeze concentrations and cryoprotectants.

StemCell Protocol

Step Action
1 Freeze HPCs on Day 12 in CryoStor CS10 at 1×10⁶ cells/mL.
2 To thaw: thaw cells in DMEM/F-12, collect the cell pellet, then plate into Microglia Differentiation Medium at 1.1×10⁴–2.2×10⁴ cells/cm² (containing 10 µM ROCK inhibitor (ROCKi) for the first feed only).

McQuade Protocol

Step Action
1 HPCs must be frozen at 2–4×10⁶ cells/mL in BamBanker (Wako).
2 If frozen, thaw HPCs directly into microglial differentiation medium and plate on Matrigel-coated plates at 10,000 cells/cm².
TipNote on thaw density

More viable when greater densities of HPCs are thawed together (McQuade Protocol note).

### Passaging cells as aggregates & setting up HPC differentiation

1. Prepare Matrigel-coated plates (see [iPSC culture protocol](ipsc-culture-protocol.qmd)).
2. Perform triplicate aggregate counts: draw a "+" counting grid centered on the bottom of 3 wells of a 96-well flat-bottom plate. Aliquot 40 µL DMEM/F12 into each of the 3 wells, then add 5 µL of aggregate mixture to each. Count aggregates ≥50 µm diameter in each well; average the triplicate to get the **Concentration of Cell Aggregates**.
3. Plate 96–196 aggregates/well (10–20 aggregates/cm²) to achieve 40–80 colonies/well (4–10 colonies/cm²) adhered after 24 h — multiple plating densities may need to be tested.

::: {.callout-tip collapse="true"}
## Plating volume calculator
$$Plating\ Volume\ (\mu L) = \frac{Number\ of\ Aggregates\ to\ Plate}{Concentration\ of\ Cell\ Aggregates\ (aggregates/\mu L)}$$
:::

```{ojs}
//| echo: false
viewof n_aggregates_to_plate = Inputs.number({label: "Number of aggregates to plate (source range: 96–196)", value: 146, min: 96, max: 196, step: 1})
viewof aggregate_conc_per_uL = Inputs.number({label: "Your measured concentration of cell aggregates (aggregates/µL, from the triplicate count above)", value: 5, min: 0.1, step: 0.1})
```

```{ojs}
//| echo: false
plating_volume_uL = n_aggregates_to_plate / aggregate_conc_per_uL

md`**Plating volume: ${fmt(plating_volume_uL,1)} µL** of aggregate mixture per well — add to a Matrigel-coated 12-well plate containing 1 mL mTeSR1. If using split ratios instead of a counted plating volume, 1:40 to 1:200 may be required depending on confluence.`
```

4. Gently mix the aggregate mixture; add the calculated plating volume to each Matrigel-coated well (1 mL mTeSR1 already in the well).
5. Place in the 37°C incubator; move in short back-and-forth/side-to-side motions to distribute aggregates; do not disturb for 24 h. Change media with mTeSR1 after 24 h; do not disturb for another 24 h. Count to confirm aggregates after 48 h.

| Day | Step |
|-----|------|
| 0 | Confirm 80–160 colonies/well adhered (4–10 colonies/cm²) — count all colonies, including tiny few-cell ones. To ease counting, replace medium with fresh mTeSR™1/mTeSR™ Plus/TeSR™-E8 first (removes debris). **CRITICAL: do not proceed if < 40 or > 160 colonies/well** (see discrepancy note above) — differentiation will be compromised. |
| 0 | Aspirate medium; add 2 mL Medium A/well. Incubate 37°C for 2 days. |
| 2 | Using a serological or 1 mL pipette, gently remove 1 mL medium/well; discard. Gently add 1 mL Medium A/well. Incubate 37°C for 24 h. |

### Stage 2 — Day 3–12

| Day | Step |
|-----|------|
| 3 | Aspirate medium; gently add 2 mL Medium B/well. Incubate 37°C for 2 days. |
| 5 | Gently remove 1 mL medium/well; discard. Gently add 1 mL Medium B/well. Incubate 37°C for 2 days. |
| 7 | Floating cells become visible and increase for the rest of the protocol. Keeping the plate flat, gently remove 1 mL medium/well without disturbing the floating population; discard. Add 1 mL Medium B/well. Incubate 37°C for 3 days. |
| 10 | Keeping the plate flat, gently remove 1 mL medium/well; discard. Add 1 mL Medium B/well. Incubate 37°C for 2 days. |
| 12 | **Harvest hematopoietic cells** (see below). |

## Optional Day 10 harvest
Cells may be harvested at Day 10 instead, as described for Day 12. Yield and the proportion of CD34+CD45+ cells will be lower than at Day 12, but CFUs may be present at a higher frequency.
:::

### Day 12 — Harvest hematopoietic cells

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Vigorously pipette (triturate) cells up/down in the well to break them up as needed. |
| 2 | Transfer the cell suspension to a collection tube. |
| 3 | Add 1 mL DMEM/F-12 to the well; triturate vigorously and add to the collection tube. |
| 4 | Repeat step 3. |
| 5 | Centrifuge 300×g, 5 min, RT. Discard the supernatant. |
| 6 | Resuspend the cell pellet in the desired medium for analysis or downstream assays. |

## Freezing hematopoietic progenitor cells

Two methods are given in the source — present both, since they specify different freeze concentrations and cryoprotectants.

### StemCell Protocol

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Freeze HPCs on Day 12 in CryoStor CS10 at **1×10⁶ cells/mL**. |
| 2 | To thaw: thaw cells in DMEM/F-12, collect the cell pellet, then plate into Microglia Differentiation Medium at 1.1×10⁴–2.2×10⁴ cells/cm² (containing 10 µM ROCK inhibitor (ROCKi) for the first feed only). |

### McQuade Protocol

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | HPCs must be frozen at **2–4×10⁶ cells/mL** in BamBanker (Wako). |
| 2 | If frozen, thaw HPCs directly into microglial differentiation medium and plate on Matrigel-coated plates at **10,000 cells/cm²**. |

::: {.callout-tip collapse="true"}
## Note on thaw density
More viable when greater densities of HPCs are thawed together (McQuade Protocol note).
:::
