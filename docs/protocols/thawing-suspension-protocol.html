Thawing THP-1 & K562 Suspension Cultures

From liquid nitrogen storage

Published

September 9, 2026

NoteOverview

THP-1 and K562 cryovials are thawed in a 37°C water bath, diluted into fresh RPMI 1640 media, pelleted to remove residual Dimethyl Sulfoxide (DMSO), and transferred to a T25 flask to recover. The two source protocols (Thawing_K562.docx, Thawing_THP1.docx) are otherwise identical — merged here into one page with a cell-line toggle.

ImportantBefore starting
  • Warm RPMI/FBS media in a 37°C water bath.
  • Keep the cryovial on ice while transferring it from the liquid nitrogen (LN₂) container to the water bath.
  • Thaw just until mostly thawed, with some ice still remaining, then remove immediately.

TipFBS vs. FCS

The THP-1 source document labels its media recipe “RPMI/FCS” (Fetal Calf Serum) rather than “RPMI/FBS” — FCS and Fetal Bovine Serum (FBS) are synonyms for the same reagent (Thermo Fisher Gibco™ 10500064 in both source protocols). Standardized to FBS throughout this page.

RPMI/FBS media

NoteMath audit

Base recipe: 500 mL RPMI + 50 mL FBS (10%) + 5 mL Penicillin/Streptomycin (0.9%), 555 mL total — scales linearly:

\[V_{RPMI} = V_{batch} \times \frac{500}{555} \qquad V_{FBS} = V_{batch} \times \frac{50}{555} \qquad V_{P/S} = V_{batch} \times \frac{5}{555}\]

Code 
media_rpmi_mL = media_batch_mL * (500 / 555)
media_fbs_mL  = media_batch_mL * (50 / 555)
media_ps_mL   = media_batch_mL * (5 / 555)

Inputs.table([
  {Component: "RPMI 1640 w/ L-Glutamine-Bicarbonate",  "Volume (mL)": fmt(media_rpmi_mL)},
  {Component: "Fetal Bovine Serum (FBS)",                "Volume (mL)": fmt(media_fbs_mL)},
  {Component: "Penicillin/Streptomycin (100X)",          "Volume (mL)": fmt(media_ps_mL)},
  {Component: "Total",                                    "Volume (mL)": fmt(media_batch_mL, 0)}
], {layout: "auto"})

Protocol (~20 min + monitoring)

Code 
cell_line === "THP-1"
  ? md`::: {.callout-tip}
## THP-1: keep the vial on ice during transfer
Get a bucket of ice to keep the cryogenic vial on when transferring it from the LN₂ container to the water bath. (This step is specific to the THP-1 source protocol — not stated for K562, but there's no reason not to apply it to either line.)
:::`
  : md``
Step Action
1 Warm media in 37°C water bath
2 Thaw cryogenic storage vial in water bath — remove once mostly thawed but some ice still remains
3 Carefully open vial; add cells to 9 mL fresh media in a 15 mL conical tube; gently pipette to mix
4 Spin 1000 RPM, 5 min; discard supernatant
5 Add 10 mL fresh media
6 Transfer to a T25 flask
7 Take a small sample for cell counting
8 Place T25 flask in incubator; monitor growth
9 Split / add fresh media once culture reaches ~1 × 10⁶ cells/mL
Code 
md`::: {.callout-important}
## Checklist
- [ ] Cell line: **${cell_line}** — confirmed on vial label
- [ ] Post-thaw count: _______ cells/mL, _______ % viability
- [ ] Keep culture between **4 × 10⁵ and 1 × 10⁶ cells/mL** for optimal growth
:::`
