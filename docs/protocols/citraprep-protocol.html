Citrate/iodixanol density-gradient nuclei prep for single-nucleus applications

Published

September 9, 2026

NoteOverview

CitraPrep isolates intact nuclei from snap-frozen tissue for downstream single-nucleus work (Step 15: “Load for single cell”). Tissue is dounce-homogenized in a citrate-based homogenization buffer (CitraHB), then nuclei are purified on an iodixanol step gradient in an ultracentrifuge (18 min scheduled run time, ~1 hr total with load/accel/no-brake coast, 10,000×g, no brake) and collected from the gradient interface. RNase inhibitors (SUPERase•In™ RNase Inhibitor + RNasin® Ribonuclease Inhibitor) go into every buffer, including the final nuclei resuspension buffer. Spermine, spermidine, and dithiothreitol (DTT), by contrast, are deliberately left out of the nuclei resuspension buffer because they interfere with downstream cDNA synthesis.

ImportantBefore starting
  • Use cell culture grade water for every “water” call in this protocol (stock buffers and 1000X fresh solutions alike), not general lab ddH₂O.
  • Make 1 M Tricine–Potassium Hydroxide (Tricine-KOH, pH 7.8) and 1 M Citric Acid stocks first; CitraHB and Diluent are both built from them.
  • Prepare CitraHB, Diluent, and Detergent stock buffers a day ahead; good for 1 month at 4°C.
  • Pre-chill the 7 mL glass dounce homogenizer on ice.
  • Make 1 M Dithiothreitol (DTT), 0.15 M spermine, and 0.5 M spermidine (1000X stocks) fresh each time.
  • Make CitraHB Complete (stock + protease inhibitor tablet) fresh; add spermine/spermidine/DTT to bring it to 1X.
  • Make the nuclei resuspension buffer during the centrifugation step (Step 8) with no spermine, spermidine, or DTT in this one.
  • Set the ultracentrifuge to 4°C and confirm the rotor/tubes are rated for no-brake deceleration (Step 8).
  • Book more time than the spin itself: the 18 min is only the run; loading, vacuum draw-down, acceleration, and the unbraked coast to a stop routinely bring the whole ultracentrifuge step to around an hour. Schedule accordingly.

Working solutions: scaled to sample count

NoteMath audit: how every volume is calculated

CitraHB Complete (10 mL aliquot per sample, used in Steps 2, 3, and 6):

\[V_{aliquot} = 10\;mL \times N_{samples} \times (1 + overage)\]

Additives (SUPERase, RNAsin, spermidine, spermine, DTT) scale linearly with \(V_{aliquot}\); protease inhibitor tablets are whole units, so the tablet count rounds up.

Iodixanol step gradient: each of the three working solutions is made by diluting 50% iodixanol (itself made 5 parts OptiPrep : 1 part Diluent, since OptiPrep ships at 60% and \(5/6 \times 60\% = 50\%\)) down to the target percentage with CitraPrepHB, using \(C_1V_1 = C_2V_2\):

\[V_{50\%\ stock\ needed} = V_{total} \times \frac{\%_{target}}{50\%} \qquad V_{diluent} = V_{total} - V_{50\%\ stock\ needed}\]

Per-sample total volumes: 15 mL of 50% (kept as-is), 4 mL of 40%, and 7.5 mL of 30%. All three scale with \(N_{samples} \times (1+overage)\).

Polyamine additives in each gradient solution scale as \(V_{total}/1000\) (spermidine, spermine, DTT, all 1000X). SUPERase and RNAsin scale as:

\[V_{stock} = \frac{C_{final}}{C_{stock}} \times V_{total}\]

Each inhibitor’s \(C_{final}\) and the shared \(C_{stock}\) are set in Configure experiment (superase_final, rnasin_final, rnase_stock; defaults 0.5 / 0.5 / 40 U/µL). Change them there and every table below rescales.

Nuclei resuspension buffer (30 mL aliquot per sample):

\[V_{buffer} = 30\;mL \times N_{samples} \times (1+overage)\]

Bovine Serum Albumin (BSA) scales as 1% w/v of \(V_{buffer}\); SUPERase and RNAsin use the same \(C_{final}/C_{stock} \times V_{buffer}\) formula above. No spermine, spermidine, or Dithiothreitol (DTT); these interfere with downstream cDNA synthesis.

Iodixanol step gradient

WarningMake base 50% first, split it, then add additives to each tube separately

The 40% and 30% solutions are diluted from additive-free base 50% iodixanol, not from the finished 50% solution in the first table below. Work in this order:

  1. Make base 50% iodixanol with OptiPrep + Diluent only. No spermidine, spermine, DTT, SUPERase, or RNAsin yet.
  2. Pull off the “50% iodixanol (above)” volumes listed in the 40% and 30% tables. That aliquot carries no additives with it.
  3. You now have three separate tubes (leftover 50%, 40% mix, 30% mix). Add spermidine, spermine, DTT, SUPERase, and RNAsin to each tube, scaled to that tube’s own total volume.

Each tube is dosed once, on its own. The RNase inhibitor in the 40% and 30% tables is not a repeat of the 50%; the 50%-derived iodixanol in those tubes started out additive-free.

Protocol

Tissue homogenization (~30 min)

TipCheck nuclei before proceeding

Step 4 calls for pulling an aliquot to check nuclei release after homogenization; add more strokes with the tight pestle if intact cells are still visible before moving on.

Step Action Volume
1 Pre-chill 7 mL glass dounce homogenizer on ice —
2 Cut snap-frozen tissue into small pieces (<0.5 cm) with clean scissors, in a petri dish on ice, in CitraHB complete 1 mL/sample
3 Transfer to dounce homogenizer on ice; add CitraHB complete; incubate 7 min on ice 4 mL/sample
4 Homogenize 20× with loose pestle; add Detergent; homogenize 20× with tight pestle. Pull an aliquot to check nuclei; homogenize further if needed 320 µL/sample Detergent
5 Filter through 40 µM strainer —

Iodixanol gradient centrifugation (~1 hr + spin)

WarningNo brake on the Step 8 spin

This spin is run on an ultracentrifuge: 18 min at 10,000×g at 4°C with no brake. Braking will disturb the gradient interface and mix layers together, ruining nuclei recovery. Schedule the ultracentrifuge for 18 min run time, but budget closer to an hour for the step overall; loading/vacuum draw-down, acceleration, and the unbraked coast to a stop add substantial time beyond the programmed 18 min.

Step Action Volume
6 Add 50% iodixanol solution to the filtered sample (5 mL CitraHB fraction) and vortex to mix 5 mL/sample
7 Underlay sample with 30% and then 40% iodixanol solutions Per gradient volumes above
8 Weigh conical tubes against each other on a scale, topping up the lighter tube(s) with cell culture grade water dropwise until matched (within ~0.1 g), then place balanced tubes opposite each other in the rotor; centrifuge in the ultracentrifuge for 18 min at 10,000×g, 4°C, no brake —
9 Collect nuclei from the interface layer with a P1000 pipette; transfer to a new tube —
ImportantChecklist

Nuclei wash & counting (~30 min)

Step Action Volume
10 Add nuclei wash buffer in a 15 mL tube; vortex 10 mL/sample
11 Filter through 20 µM filter —
12 Spin 10 min at 500×g, 4°C to pellet nuclei —
13 Resuspend in nuclei wash buffer; repeat spin (Step 12) 10 mL/sample
14 Resuspend in nuclei wash buffer; count using hemocytometer (see method below) 200 µL/sample
15 Load for single cell —
TipHemocytometer counting method

CitraPrep nuclei yields are typically too low for a reliable Countess (automated) read, so this is a manual hemocytometer count every time; do not substitute the Countess.

  1. Filter your trypan blue stock through a 0.2 µm filter before use; unfiltered trypan blue carries particulates that get miscounted as nuclei, and this matters more at low nuclei numbers where a few false counts skew the result.
  2. Mix the nuclei suspension with filtered trypan blue at a 1:1 ratio (e.g. 10 µL suspension + 10 µL trypan blue, dilution factor = 2).
  3. Load 10 µL of the mix onto the hemocytometer; let it wick in by capillary action, no bubbles.
  4. Under the microscope, count nuclei in the 4 corner grid squares (16 small squares each). Use the standard hemocytometer counting rule: count nuclei touching the top and left borders of a square, but not the bottom or right, to avoid double-counting nuclei that sit on a line.
  5. Calculate concentration:

\[Nuclei/mL = \frac{\text{sum of nuclei in 4 corner squares}}{4} \times 2\ (\text{dilution factor}) \times 10^4\]

Divide by 1000 to convert to nuclei/µL for loading calculations.

| 0.15 M spermine | 1000X | 0.03035 g in 1 mL cell culture grade water |
| 0.5 M spermidine | 1000X | 0.072625 g in 1 mL cell culture grade water |

Add spermine, spermidine, and Dithiothreitol (DTT) to all buffers below for a final 1X concentration, **except** the nuclei resuspension buffer (see warning below).

Add RNase inhibitor to all buffers below at the final concentrations set in **Configure experiment** above. The defaults are 0.5 U/µL SUPERase•In™ RNase Inhibitor (SUPERase) and 0.5 U/µL RNasin® Ribonuclease Inhibitor (RNAsin), 1 U/µL total. Different lab members dose these differently; change the values up top and every table below rescales (set one to 0 to leave it out). Both are referred to by their short names (SUPERase, RNAsin) throughout the rest of this document.

## Working solutions: scaled to sample count

::: {.callout-note collapse="true"}
## Math audit: how every volume is calculated

**CitraHB Complete** (10 mL aliquot per sample, used in Steps 2, 3, and 6):

$$V_{aliquot} = 10\;mL \times N_{samples} \times (1 + overage)$$

Additives (SUPERase, RNAsin, spermidine, spermine, DTT) scale linearly with $V_{aliquot}$; protease inhibitor tablets are whole units, so the tablet count rounds up.

**Iodixanol step gradient**: each of the three working solutions is made by diluting 50% iodixanol (itself made 5 parts OptiPrep : 1 part Diluent, since OptiPrep ships at 60% and $5/6 \times 60\% = 50\%$) down to the target percentage with CitraPrepHB, using $C_1V_1 = C_2V_2$:

$$V_{50\%\ stock\ needed} = V_{total} \times \frac{\%_{target}}{50\%} \qquad V_{diluent} = V_{total} - V_{50\%\ stock\ needed}$$

Per-sample total volumes: 15 mL of 50% (kept as-is), 4 mL of 40%, and 7.5 mL of 30%. All three scale with $N_{samples} \times (1+overage)$.

Polyamine additives in each gradient solution scale as $V_{total}/1000$ (spermidine, spermine, DTT, all 1000X). SUPERase and RNAsin scale as:

$$V_{stock} = \frac{C_{final}}{C_{stock}} \times V_{total}$$

Each inhibitor's $C_{final}$ and the shared $C_{stock}$ are set in **Configure experiment** (`superase_final`, `rnasin_final`, `rnase_stock`; defaults 0.5 / 0.5 / 40 U/µL). Change them there and every table below rescales.

<!-- TODO: SUPERase/RNAsin in the retained 50% fraction are dosed at half the rate of the 40%/30% fractions (divided by 2). Preserved here as-is; confirm with JP whether this is intentional before scaling to large batches. -->

**Nuclei resuspension buffer** (30 mL aliquot per sample):

$$V_{buffer} = 30\;mL \times N_{samples} \times (1+overage)$$

Bovine Serum Albumin (BSA) scales as 1% w/v of $V_{buffer}$; SUPERase and RNAsin use the same $C_{final}/C_{stock} \times V_{buffer}$ formula above. No spermine, spermidine, or Dithiothreitol (DTT); these interfere with downstream cDNA synthesis.
:::

The 40% and 30% solutions are diluted from **additive-free base 50% iodixanol**, not from the finished 50% solution in the first table below. Work in this order:

1. Make base 50% iodixanol with **OptiPrep + Diluent only.** No spermidine, spermine, DTT, SUPERase, or RNAsin yet.

## Protocol

### Tissue homogenization (~30 min)

::: {.callout-tip collapse="true"}
## Check nuclei before proceeding
Step 4 calls for pulling an aliquot to check nuclei release after homogenization; add more strokes with the tight pestle if intact cells are still visible before moving on.
:::

| Step | Action | Volume |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Pre-chill 7 mL glass dounce homogenizer on ice | — |
| 2 | Cut snap-frozen tissue into small pieces (<0.5 cm) with clean scissors, in a petri dish on ice, in CitraHB complete | 1 mL/sample |
| 3 | Transfer to dounce homogenizer on ice; add CitraHB complete; incubate 7 min on ice | 4 mL/sample |
| 4 | Homogenize 20× with loose pestle; add Detergent; homogenize 20× with tight pestle. Pull an aliquot to check nuclei; homogenize further if needed | 320 µL/sample Detergent |
| 5 | Filter through 40 µM strainer | — |

### Iodixanol gradient centrifugation (~1 hr + spin)

::: {.callout-warning}
## No brake on the Step 8 spin
This spin is run on an **ultracentrifuge**: **18 min at 10,000×g at 4°C with no brake**. Braking will disturb the gradient interface and mix layers together, ruining nuclei recovery. Schedule the ultracentrifuge for **18 min** run time, but budget closer to **an hour** for the step overall; loading/vacuum draw-down, acceleration, and the unbraked coast to a stop add substantial time beyond the programmed 18 min.
:::

| Step | Action | Volume |
|------|--------|--------|
| 6 | Add 50% iodixanol solution to the filtered sample (5 mL CitraHB fraction) and vortex to mix | 5 mL/sample |
| 7 | Underlay sample with 30% and then 40% iodixanol solutions | Per gradient volumes above |
| 8 | Weigh conical tubes against each other on a scale, topping up the lighter tube(s) with cell culture grade water dropwise until matched (within ~0.1 g), then place balanced tubes opposite each other in the rotor; centrifuge in the ultracentrifuge for 18 min at 10,000×g, 4°C, **no brake** | — |
| 9 | Collect nuclei from the interface layer with a P1000 pipette; transfer to a new tube | — |

::: {.callout-important}
## Checklist
- [ ] Gradient tubes weighed against each other and balanced to within ~0.1 g before spinning
- [ ] Balanced tubes loaded opposite each other in the rotor
- [ ] Ultracentrifuge confirmed at no-brake setting
- [ ] Interface layer collected cleanly, minimal carryover of gradient layers
:::

::: {.callout-tip}
## Hemocytometer counting method
CitraPrep nuclei yields are typically too low for a reliable Countess (automated) read, so this is a **manual hemocytometer count** every time; do not substitute the Countess.

a. Filter your trypan blue stock through a 0.2 µm filter before use; unfiltered trypan blue carries particulates that get miscounted as nuclei, and this matters more at low nuclei numbers where a few false counts skew the result.
b. Mix the nuclei suspension with filtered trypan blue at a **1:1** ratio (e.g. 10 µL suspension + 10 µL trypan blue, dilution factor = 2).
c. Load 10 µL of the mix onto the hemocytometer; let it wick in by capillary action, no bubbles.
d. Under the microscope, count nuclei in the 4 corner grid squares (16 small squares each). Use the standard hemocytometer counting rule: count nuclei touching the top and left borders of a square, but not the bottom or right, to avoid double-counting nuclei that sit on a line.
e. Calculate concentration:

$$Nuclei/mL = \frac{\text{sum of nuclei in 4 corner squares}}{4} \times 2\ (\text{dilution factor}) \times 10^4$$

Divide by 1000 to convert to nuclei/µL for loading calculations.
:::

::: {.callout-important}
## Checklist
- [ ] Trypan blue filtered (0.2 µm) before use
- [ ] Nuclei suspension diluted 1:1 with trypan blue
- [ ] Nuclei intact and free of debris under the hemocytometer
- [ ] Nuclei concentration: _______ nuclei/µL
- [ ] Loaded for single-cell run at: _______
:::
