QIAamp DNA Mini Kit (Qiagen, cat #51304) — cultured cell input

Published

September 9, 2026

NoteOverview

Isolates genomic DNA from cultured cells (source protocol notes THP1 media on refeed) using the QIAamp DNA Mini Kit: Proteinase K + Buffer AL lysis, ethanol precipitation conditioning, spin-column binding, two washes (Buffer AW1, AW2), and water elution. No stock solutions need to be prepared ahead of time — every reagent ships ready-to-use in the kit.

ImportantBefore starting
  • Turn on the heat block to 56°C.
  • Confirm 96–100% ethanol is on hand (not included in the kit).
  • Have DNA LoBind microcentrifuge tubes ready for the final elution step.

TipWhy overage on kit buffers at all?

Buffer AL, AW1, AW2, and Proteinase K are ready-to-use kit reagents added to individual tubes/columns, not pooled into a shared master mix. The totals below are for planning/aliquoting purposes (how much to pull from the stock bottle for a batch of N samples) rather than a mix you physically combine — pipetting loss across repeated aliquoting is the reason for the overage, same convention as this notebook’s other reagent tables. Buffer AW1, AW2, and elution water are not pooled ahead of time in the source protocol; the totals for those below assume you may want to pre-aliquot rather than pipette straight from the stock bottle per column.

Reagent volumes

NoteMath audit — how the totals are calculated

All per-column reagent volumes are stated directly in the source protocol (not derived): 20 µL Proteinase K, 200 µL Buffer AL, 200 µL ethanol, 500 µL Buffer AW1, 500 µL Buffer AW2, 100 µL elution water — all per sample, fixed regardless of cell input. Batch totals for ordering/pre-aliquoting scale as:

\[V_{total} = V_{per\_sample} \times N_{samples} \times (1+overage)\]

Elution water is kept exact (×N only, no overage) since eluate volume is precise per column and any water not fully used is simply discarded — no benefit to over-preparing it.

  {Step: "4",  Action: "Aspirate supernatant",                                                                        Detail: "—"},

  {Step: "6",  Action: "Add 20 µL QIAGEN Proteinase K",                                                               Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "7",  Action: "Add 200 µL Buffer AL; mix by pulse-vortexing 15 sec",                                        Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "8",  Action: "Incubate at 56°C for 10 min",                                                                 Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "9",  Action: "Briefly centrifuge tube",                                                                     Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "10", Action: "Add 200 µL ethanol (96–100%); mix by pulse-vortexing 15 sec",                                Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "11", Action: "Briefly centrifuge tube",                                                                     Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "12", Action: "Apply mixture to QIAamp Mini spin column (2 mL collection tube), without wetting rim",       Detail: "Close cap; centrifuge 8000 rpm (6000×g), 1 min"},
  {Step: "13", Action: "Place column in clean 2 mL collection tube; discard filtrate tube",                          Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "14", Action: "Add 500 µL Buffer AW1 without wetting rim; close cap",                                       Detail: "Centrifuge 8000 rpm (6000×g), 1 min"},
  {Step: "15", Action: "Place column in clean 2 mL collection tube; discard filtrate tube",                          Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "16", Action: "Add 500 µL Buffer AW2 without wetting rim; close cap",                                       Detail: "Centrifuge full speed (14,000 rpm), 3 min"},
  {Step: "17", Action: "Place column in new 1.5 mL tube; discard old collection tube",                               Detail: "Centrifuge full speed, 1 min (dry spin)"},
  {Step: "18", Action: "Place column in a clean 1.5 mL DNA LoBind tube; discard filtrate tube",                      Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "19", Action: "Add 100 µL nuclease-free water without wetting rim",                                         Detail: "—"},
  {Step: "20", Action: "Incubate at RT for 5 min",                                                                    Detail: "Then centrifuge 8000 rpm (6000×g), 2 min"},
  {Step: "21", Action: "Quantify DNA using Qubit",                                                                    Detail: "—"}
], {layout: "auto"})
WarningDo not wet the column rim

Buffers AW1, AW2, and the final elution water must all be pipetted directly onto the membrane without touching the rim of the spin column — buffer trapped in the rim threads can carry over into the eluate and interfere with downstream enzymatic reactions.
