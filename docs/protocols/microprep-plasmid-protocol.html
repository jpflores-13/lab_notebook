Fast alkaline-lysis miniprep for 1 mL cultures · 2-day protocol

Published

September 9, 2026

NoteOverview

A fast, small-scale alkaline-lysis plasmid prep (“microprep”) from 1 mL overnight bacterial cultures — no spin-column kit required. Cells are lysed with Solutions I/II/III, plasmid DNA is cleared with two chloroform extractions, precipitated with ethanol, dried, and resuspended in a small volume of water. Runs over 2 days: Day 1 inoculate and grow overnight; Day 2 microprep.

ImportantBefore starting
  • Day 1: inoculate 1 mL LB media (with the appropriate antibiotic) per clone from a single CFU; grow overnight at 37°C, 200 rpm.
  • Reserve 50 µL of each overnight culture in a separate tube as a glycerol-stock source before proceeding — the rest goes into the microprep.
  • Make Solution II fresh immediately before use.
  • Pre-chill absolute ethanol at −20°C.
  • Set the dry bath to 55°C for the drying step.

Configure microprep run

Code 
viewof n_samples   = Inputs.number({label: "Number of cultures (clones)", value: 8, min: 1, max: 96, step: 1})
viewof excess_pct  = Inputs.number({label: "Reagent overage (%)", value: 10, min: 0, max: 30, step: 5})
Code 
scale = n_samples * (1 + excess_pct / 100)
fmt   = (x, d = 1) => x.toFixed(d)

md`**${n_samples} culture(s) · ${excess_pct}% overage · scale factor ${fmt(scale, 2)}×**`
NoteMath audit — how every volume is calculated

Every reagent below is dispensed per-tube at a fixed per-sample volume, so total quantity to have on hand scales linearly:

\[V_{total} = V_{per\_sample} \times N_{samples} \times (1 + overage)\]

Only Solution I + RNase A (added together and vortexed in Step 4) is realistically premixed as a shared master mix — Solutions II and III, chloroform, and ethanol are added directly per-tube during the prep, so the table below is an ordering/prep quantity calculator rather than a single combined master mix.

Ethanol precipitation volume is calculated as a configurable fraction (0.8–1×) of the ~450 µL supernatant volume carried through the two chloroform extractions, per the source protocol’s “0.8-1 volume” instruction.

Protocol

Step Action Volume
1 Inoculate LB media with a pure CFU (from LB agar or frozen glycerol stock) + antibiotic; incubate overnight at 37°C, 200 rpm 1 mL LB + 10 µL antibiotic
2 Reserve 50 µL of culture in a separate autoclaved tube for future glycerol-stock purposes; use the rest for the microprep 0.5 mL tube
3 Centrifuge at maximum speed for 2 min; discard supernatant —
4 Resuspend the pellet by vortexing in Solution I + RNase A 100 µL Solution I + 1 µL RNase A
5 Add fresh Solution II; mix gently 200 µL
6 Add Solution III; mix gently 150 µL
7 Centrifuge for 10 min at maximum speed —
8 Transfer the supernatant to a new tube with 1 volume of chloroform; mix gently; centrifuge 5 min at maximum speed ~450 µL chloroform
9 Repeat the chloroform extraction (Step 8) ~450 µL chloroform
10 Transfer the supernatant to a new tube with 0.8–1 volume of absolute ethanol; mix gently; incubate at −20°C for 20 min 0.8–1× supernatant vol (see reagent table above)
11 Centrifuge for 5 min at maximum speed; discard supernatant —
12 A small white pellet may be visible at this point; if not, continue and check DNA concentration at the end —
13 Dry slowly at 55°C in a dry bath —
14 Resuspend the pellet in bidistilled water; store at −20°C 20 µL
TipNo visible pellet after Step 11?

The source protocol notes this is not necessarily a failure — continue the prep and check the DNA concentration (e.g., Nanodrop/Qubit) at the end before troubleshooting.
