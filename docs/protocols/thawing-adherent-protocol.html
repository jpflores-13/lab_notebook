Thawing Protocol — Adherent Cells

  • Show All Code

From liquid nitrogen storage

Published

September 9, 2026

NoteOverview

General thawing protocol for adherent cell lines stored in liquid nitrogen (LN₂). Cells are thawed in a 37°C bath, diluted into fresh complete media, pelleted to remove residual Dimethyl Sulfoxide (DMSO), and plated into a 100 mm culture dish.

ImportantBefore starting
  • Warm complete media in a 37°C water bath or bead bath. If only a bead bath is available, add a container of water and warm it to 37°C as well.
  • Prepare complete media stock under the hood.
  • Spray the cryovial with ethanol before opening it in the culture hood.

Complete media (10% FBS, 1% Penicillin/Streptomycin)

NoteMath audit

Base recipe: 500 mL base media + 50 mL FBS (10%) + 5 mL Penicillin/Streptomycin (0.9%), 555 mL total — scales linearly:

\[V_{base} = V_{batch} \times \frac{500}{555} \qquad V_{FBS} = V_{batch} \times \frac{50}{555} \qquad V_{P/S} = V_{batch} \times \frac{5}{555}\]

Code 
media_base_mL = media_batch_mL * (500 / 555)
media_fbs_mL  = media_batch_mL * (50 / 555)
media_ps_mL   = media_batch_mL * (5 / 555)

Inputs.table([
  {Component: `Base media (${base_media})`,              "Volume (mL)": fmt(media_base_mL)},
  {Component: "Fetal Bovine Serum (FBS)",                  "Volume (mL)": fmt(media_fbs_mL)},
  {Component: "Penicillin/Streptomycin (100X)",            "Volume (mL)": fmt(media_ps_mL)},
  {Component: "Total",                                      "Volume (mL)": fmt(media_batch_mL, 0)}
], {layout: "auto"})

Prepare under the hood.

Protocol (~20 min)

Step Action
1 Warm media in 37°C water or bead bath
2 Thaw cryogenic storage vial in water bath — remove once mostly thawed but some ice still remains
3 Spray vial with ethanol; carefully open and add cells to 9 mL fresh media in a 15 mL conical tube; gently pipette to mix
4 Spin 300×g, 5 min; discard supernatant
5 Add 10 mL fresh media
6 Transfer to a 100 mm dish
NoteSource typo, fixed here

The source document reads “Transfer to the 10mm dish,” which conflicts with the dish listed in Materials & Reagents (100 mm Corning TC-Treated Culture Dishes). Corrected to 100 mm dish above.
